Our Story

THE JOURNEY
BEGINS

THE PROBLEM

Technical interviews are notoriously challenging, yet most preparation resources are either expensive, outdated, or fail to provide actionable feedback. Engineers deserve better.

THE SOLUTION

JobSage was conceived as a free, AI-powered platform that provides instant, expert-level feedback on system design interviews, making elite preparation accessible to everyone.

THE MISSION

To revolutionize how engineers prepare for technical interviews by democratizing access to world-class preparation tools and personalized AI feedback.
